ABOUT OUR PROJECT

Expense Tracker

Track Better. Spend Smarter. Save More.

Expense Tracker is a personal finance management application designed to help users monitor their income, manage daily expenses, plan budgets and understand their spending habits.

Our goal is to make financial management simple, organized and accessible through an easy-to-use digital platform.
